# Kickoff Script — Workshop Step 2 (Declare the Failure)

The exact words the facilitator reads aloud at workshop step 2. Going **big** (complete
disaster, not a minor slip) is deliberate — it widens the search and surfaces tail
risks. Swap in the real project name and a concrete worst-credible outcome.

---

> "Imagine it is **[DATE + horizon — e.g. one year from today]**.
>
> We executed **[PROJECT / DECISION]** exactly as written. We followed the plan to
> the letter.
>
> The outcome was a **complete disaster**. [Name the worst-credible outcome:
> *we shipped late and missed the window* / *the migration corrupted data and we
> rolled back publicly* / *customers churned and the product was killed* /
> *reputation damage we're still recovering from*].
>
> It has **failed**. Not 'it struggled' — it failed.
>
> Take the next **5–10 minutes**. Working **silently and on your own**, write down
> in **past tense** every reason you can think of for *why it failed*. Don't
> filter, don't rank, don't hold back the uncomfortable ones. Go for **quantity**.
>
> Remember: this already happened. You're not predicting — you're explaining."

---

**Facilitator reminders**
- Past tense only. If someone writes "what if X?", convert it to "X happened, why?".
- No discussion yet — generation now, evaluation later.
- The most junior person shares first in the round-robin; the sponsor speaks last.
